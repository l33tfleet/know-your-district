# The demo story (draft 2026-10-30, operator; Kevin corrects)

One runnable story drives the factory until Nov 16 (decisions §13). Every `front` Issue names the step it moves
forward (`Story step: N`); the digest reports "Story: k of 7 steps pass" on the live site after each deploy.
The runnable form is `.factory/story.journey` (the factory's journey.py DSL; story.py runs it). Accessible names in it are the contract for unbuilt UI.

**Who:** a staffer in the House District 22 office (Mōʻiliʻili, Honolulu), the week before a community meeting.
**What they need:** pick one outreach opportunity they can stand behind, act on it, and pick it up again later
without starting over. **Today:** census.gov tables by tract, a program list from memory, a spreadsheet nobody
updates.

| # | Situation | On screen | What it lets them decide or do | Passes when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knows the neighbourhood, not the district number | Types "Mōʻiliʻili" in the home search | Gets to the right brief in seconds | House 22 brief opens from the place name; ambiguous places offer a choice, never a guess |
| 2 | Lands on the brief | The district's outline and map with a table twin; the opening case card | Sees where and who in one glance | Map and table twin visible; case card shows a cited figure with its margin |
| 3 | Has a question in their own words | Asks "Where could food-assistance outreach help here?" | The page reorganises around the group the question is about | The brief shows the matching group's figures and case; every number from the cited tools; low confidence asks back with options |
| 4 | Wants to justify it to the representative | The match: SNAP for households in poverty not receiving SNAP (773 ±172), why here, uncertainty, the official next step | Decides this is worth acting on | Reason, margin, "not eligibility" caution and an official link are visible together |
| 5 | Decides | Saves the case to the office folder, priority High, status Planned | Commits the office | The case is in the folder with priority and status |
| 6 | Acts | Adds a dated note: "Table at Mōʻiliʻili meeting 11/8" | Records progress | The note shows on the saved case, date first |
| 7 | Comes back next week (fresh browser, folder link) | Opens the folder; asks "What needs attention?" | Knows what to do next without re-researching | The saved case is listed; the attention answer names it from saved priority, status and note |

**Wow, stated as checks:** within 20 s a viewer knows who this helps and what to do; a question visibly changes the
evidence; every recommendation shows why here, uncertainty and next step; reopening changes the answer because the
office's decisions persisted; screen and print tell the same case.

**Not this story (parked until 7/7 pass):** more programs (#83–85), county contacts and email drafts (#216–219),
general suggestions (#229–232), statewide rankings or quizzes (#302).

reliable data (says so; no number).
